# Turban Legacy Online

House of Turbans

Tradition. Quality. Trust. Since 1970.

For over 50 years, House of Turbans has been a trusted name among Sikh and Punjabi families, serving generations with premium-quality turbans and dastars.

Founded by Sarnagat Singh, the store was built on the values of quality, trust, and dedicated customer service. Today, that legacy is proudly continued by Hardeep Singh, who remains committed to serving the Sikh community with the same passion and authenticity that have defined the business for decades.

Located in the heart of Mumbai at Shop Nos. 95 & 96, Mahatma Gandhi Market, King Circle, House of Turbans is recognized as one of Maharashtra's oldest and most respected dedicated turban retailers.

After more than five decades of serving customers through our physical store, we are proud to take our legacy online, making our collection accessible to Sikh communities and turban enthusiasts across India and around the world.

A Legacy of Quality

At House of Turbans, we believe that a turban is more than just a piece of fabric, it is a symbol of identity, heritage, dignity, and pride. Every fabric we stock is carefully selected to ensure comfort, durability, vibrant colours, and an elegant drape.

Our commitment to quality has earned the trust of thousands of customers over the years, many of whom have been shopping with us for generations. We take pride in offering some of the finest turban fabrics available, sourced from renowned manufacturers and trusted brands.

Our Collection

We offer a wide range of premium turban fabrics in more than 100 colours, ensuring the perfect choice for daily wear, religious occasions, weddings, celebrations, and special events.

Competitive Pricing

We are committed to providing premium quality turban fabrics at competitive prices, ensuring exceptional value without compromising on quality.

Serving Customers Worldwide

What began as a trusted family business in Mumbai has now expanded beyond geographical boundaries. Through our online platform, we aim to make high-quality turban fabrics available to customers across India and around the world, while maintaining the same personal attention and trust that have been the foundation of our business for over 50 years.
